Daniel is a nuclear engineer at Canadian Nuclear Laboratories. He worked for Ontario Power Generation, and graduated with a nuclear engineering degree from the University of Ontario Institute of Technology in 2018. He's had the honor of working with various organizations, including Google, Microsoft, and Scotiabank. He's active in the development and engineering community to help communicate valuable experiences about the effectiveness of nuclear technologies.
